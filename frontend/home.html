How to Check Property on CERSAI

Follow these steps to verify if any loan or mortgage is registered against a property

1

Visit the Official CERSAI Website

Go to https://www.cersai.org.in

2

Click on "Public Search"

On the homepage, scroll down and look for "Public Search for Security Interest" or navigate from Services > Public Search

Direct link: https://www.cersai.org.in/CERSAI/publicsearch.prg

3

Choose the Type of Search

CERSAI allows different types of search:

  • Asset-Based Search
    • Immovable Property (land, building)
    • Movable Property (vehicles, inventory)
  • Borrower-Based Search
    • Individual (PAN, name, etc.)
    • Entity (company name, CIN, etc.)
4

Fill in the Details

Example for Immovable Property Search:

  • State
  • District
  • Village/Town
  • Survey No. / Plot No.
  • Address of the Property (if available)
5

Enter CAPTCHA and Submit

Verify CAPTCHA and click on Search

6

View the Results

If there's any security interest (loan/mortgage) registered against the property, you'll see:

  • Name of Financial Institution
  • Type of charge
  • Date of charge registration
  • Property details
  • Charge amount

Note: The charge amount will be displayed in the search results, showing the total amount of the loan or mortgage registered against the property.
